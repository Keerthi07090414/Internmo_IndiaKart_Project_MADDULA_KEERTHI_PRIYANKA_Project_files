## Key findings so far

- No duplicate rows or keys, and all 7 table relationships are valid
- Payment failure rate is 3.50%; cancellation rate is 12.0%
- Electronics is 57.7% of delivered revenue
- GMV is flat (full-month GMV is 1.2% lower year over year), with no visible Diwali spike
- Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra give about 35% of all orders
- Premium customers do not have a higher average order value than other segments
